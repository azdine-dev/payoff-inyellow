# What we took from the reference sheet (HayeCreatives, Retro), and what we left

Borrowed (the feel, never the content):
1. Gridlines off on every tab, thin spacer columns (about 1.5 wide) so blocks read like cards on a page.
2. One very big tab title at the top left, then small bold section labels. One font family for body text.
3. A row of big numbers with a small label and a bar under each, and one overview row per debt with its own bar.
4. A help tab for "it opens as view only" (Drive space, work account, VPN) with a contact line and a version number.
5. A Start tab with the currency, a date format check and a "Show where to type" switch that tints the input cells.

Left out:
6. Their words, labels, cell positions, colours and formulas. Ours are written from scratch.
7. Google-only functions (SPARKLINE and friends): ours opens in Excel 2016 too.
8. About 230,000 formulas on one tab: ours stays under 40,000.
9. Monthly only, no buffer, no yearly bills, balances that skip interest: ours follows engine v2 payday by payday.
10. The retro orange, red and pink: ours is calm sage, ink, and yellow only for paid.
