What your buffer is for

A buffer is money set aside for surprises. Not for treats, and not for bills you already know about. For the car that won't start, the tooth that needs a dentist, the trip you didn't plan.

Most people who fall back into debt didn't fail. A surprise bill arrived, there was no cash, and it went on a card. Your buffer is the wall between you and that moment.

That's why your plan fills it first. Until it reaches your goal, the extra goes there. Once it's full, every extra dollar goes to your debts.

Keep it somewhere boring: a plain separate account or an envelope, with no card linked to it.
