# PaidInYellow: Competitor Summary v2 (30 Oct 2026)

Updates the 14 Oct summary after: 6 Etsy listings reviewed (AY Studios, HayeCreatives, green Debt Snowball sheet, pink Simple Debt Payoff Tracker, Ultimate Debt Payoff Tracker 2.0, My Productive Life Co), the AY app and HayeCreatives sheet bought and tested, and the Debt Payoff Planner phone app. Full teardown of AY and Haye: competitor-teardown-ay-haye.md.

## Corrections to v1
- "Nobody plans per paycheck" is no longer true: Ultimate 2.0, AY and Debt Payoff Planner offer weekly / every 2 weeks / twice a month. Still unique: nobody gives a to-do for THIS payday (bills due before next payday, then buffer, then one debt for the extra, one "I did it").
- Privacy alone is not unique (the pink sheet says "Secure & private, no sign-ups"). Ours: no bank login, works offline, numbers never leave the phone, sync without an account.
- AY "online + offline" = one HTML file using browser storage; sync needs an AY account.
- Yellow as a colour is not unique (Haye uses a yellow accent). We own the meaning: yellow = paid.

## Must-haves (buyers expect)
Snowball / avalanche / custom, auto-sorted · up to 50 debts (sheet) · any debt type · currency by country · dashboard (owed, paid, %, debt-free date, months left, interest, this period's payment) · schedule with paid periods crossed off · planned vs actual and +/- · new charges log · rollover · progress chart · phone, tablet, computer · video tutorial · payment history · promo / deferred-interest date · help opening the file · Excel compatibility (sheet).

## Openings (where they are weak)
- No to-do for this payday; all think in months or schedules.
- No buffer first, no yearly bills: the surprise bill still lands on a card.
- No couples plan.
- Too dense (sheets, AY's 14 pages) or too empty (printables).
- AY pushes balance transfers and consolidation loans; red interest numbers; on a phone the first screen is totals, not what to pay.
- HayeCreatives: Google Sheets only, no refunds; actual balances skip interest in months with a logged payment.
- Debt Payoff Planner: monthly funding, red "Past due" and red balance, paywall on basics, no next step on Home.

## Ideas we took
Planned vs actual, charges log, what-if, windfall, milestones, .ics (all); "Worth knowing" panel, take-home helper, promo/deferred warning, printable this-payday sheet (AY); "How to open" help, date-format check, version number (Haye); "Pay a bit more" table, key-number row, debts paid X of Y, months/years toggle (sheets); low-text cards, big countdown, labels, calendar view, empty states (Debt Payoff Planner).

## Deliberately not copied
Credit score and utilisation, balance transfer and consolidation tools, six methods, 14+ screens, streaks, red numbers, two product versions, sassy women-only tone, fake urgency.
