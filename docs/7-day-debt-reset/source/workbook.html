Before you start

How this works

One page a day. The PaidInYellow app does the math. You do one small thing and color a box.

You need

  • Your card and loan statements, or your banking app.
  • The PaidInYellow app open on your phone.
  • A pen and a yellow highlighter.

Three rules

  • Do today's page only. Tomorrow can wait.
  • Missed a day? Do it tomorrow. Life happens. Never miss twice.
  • Rough numbers are fine. You can fix them later.

What changes in 7 days

Today you may not know when this ends. In a week you will know your debt-free date, what to pay on your next payday, where your surprise money lives, and you will have made your first planned payment.

A note on money words

We write $ in this workbook. Read it as your own currency. Minimum is the smallest payment your statement asks for. Rate is the interest rate, shown as APR on your statement. Buffer is money set aside for surprises.

Day 3 of 7 · about 15 minutes

Give your buffer a home

Day 3

The buffer is money for surprises, so a car repair or a doctor's bill doesn't go back on a card. It's the step most plans skip, and the one that keeps you out of debt.

  1. Choose a home for it: a plain separate account at your bank or credit union, or an envelope at home.
  2. Name it so you know what it's for: Surprises or Buffer.
  3. Put in what you can today, even $10.
  4. Type the amount in the app, on Safety net.

Day 4 of 7 · about 10 minutes

Meet your plan

Day 4

The app pays your smallest debt first, unless one grows even with its minimum. A study of about 6,000 people in a debt program found that clearing small balances first made people more likely to finish.

  1. Open Path. Find your debt-free date and the first debt to go.
  2. Tap What if I add more? Add $20 each payday and watch the date move.
  3. Leave the slider where it feels honest. Tap Use this in my plan only if you can keep it up.

Why one debt at a time

Every debt gets its minimum. All the extra goes to one debt until it's gone. Then its minimum joins the extra for the next one. The amount you pay each payday stays the same, but it clears debts faster and faster.

Keep this page

Wobble? Reset.

A bad week, a charge on the card, a missed payday. It happens to almost everyone who gets out of debt. A wobble only becomes a fall if you stop.

  1. Breathe. Don't open every statement in a panic.
  2. Open the app. Put a new charge on its debt (Debts, then Add a new charge or fee), or tap Skip this payday.
  3. Read your new date. It moved a little. It didn't disappear.
  4. On your next payday, do the ritual. Never miss twice.

If the plan can't work right now

If your minimums are more than the money you have, that happens, and there are people whose job is to help. Call each lender and ask about a hardship program. In the US, a nonprofit credit counselor (NFCC.org) can help you make a plan. Avoid anyone who offers a new loan to fix it.

Life happens. Never miss twice.
