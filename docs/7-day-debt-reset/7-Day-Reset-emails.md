## Day 2

**Subject:** Day 2: find your first $500
**Preview:** It's often closer than you think.

Hi,

Turn to page 6 of your workbook: 30 ways to find money. Tick what fits your life, pick your best 5, and do one of them today.

In the app, open Safety net, then Find your first $500. Tick what you do and the total adds itself up.

## Day 3

**Subject:** Day 3: give your buffer a home
**Preview:** The step most plans skip.

Hi,

Most people fall back into debt for one reason: a surprise bill goes on a card.

Today you give your buffer a home. A plain separate account at your bank or credit union works, or an envelope at home. Name it "Surprises". Put in what you can today, even $10, and type it in the app on Safety net.

Your plan fills the buffer first. Once it's full, all the extra goes to your debts.

PaidInYellow

## Day 4

**Subject:** Day 4: meet your plan (10 minutes)
**Preview:** See which debt goes first, and why.

Hi,

Open Path in the app. You'll see your debt-free date and the first debt to go.

The app pays your smallest debt first, unless one grows even with its minimum. Clearing a small one early is what keeps people going.

Try What if I add more? Add $20 each payday and watch the date move. Keep it only if you can really do it.

Then fill in page 8 of your workbook, including your why.

PaidInYellow

## Day 5

**Subject:** Day 5: your 10-minute payday ritual
**Preview:** Decide once. Then just follow it.

Hi,

Today you decide when you'll do the plan, so you don't have to think about it again.

Finish this sentence on page 9: "When I get paid on ___, I open PaidInYellow at ___."

Then in the app, add the Payday reminder so it lands in your phone's calendar. Cut out the ritual card on page 13 and keep it in your wallet.

Every payday after this is 10 minutes: open Today, pay, move, tap I did it.

## Day 6

**Subject:** Day 6: bills that aren't really surprises
**Preview:** Car repairs, gifts, school. A little each payday.

Hi,

Some bills come every year, just not every month. Car repairs, gifts, school costs, insurance renewals.

List yours on page 10. Not sure how much? Look at what you spent last year in your banking app.

Add your top two or three in the app: Safety net, then Add a bill. The app sets aside a little each payday so they never land on a card.

Planned money is calm money.

PaidInYellow

**Subject:** Day 7: your first yellow
**Preview:** Do the ritual for real today.

Today you make your first planned payment. If your payday isn't today, do it on your payday.

Open Today, pay the bills listed, move the buffer money, send the extra to the debt the app names, and tap I did it. Watch it turn yellow.

Then color the box on page 11, and the first square on your tracker.

A week ago you may not have known when this would end. Now you have a date, a buffer and a plan for every payday.
