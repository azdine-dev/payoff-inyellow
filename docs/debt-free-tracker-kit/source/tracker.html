How to use your trackers

  1. Print only the pages you'll use. Most people start with the Payday tracker and 100 squares.
  2. Write your numbers in your own currency. Every tracker leaves the amounts blank.
  3. After each payday, tap I did it in the app, then color your squares in yellow.
  4. Put one tracker where you'll see it: the fridge, a mirror, inside a cupboard door.
